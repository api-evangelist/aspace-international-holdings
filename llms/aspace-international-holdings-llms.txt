# ASPACE International Holdings

## Business

- Abu Dhabi Space Eco City — a three-million-square-metre aerospace industrial cluster the company says will bring together more than 3,000 partner companies in satellite manufacturing, space trade and aerospace technology innovation.
- The ASPACE brand is shared with Hong Kong-listed USPACE Technology Group, whose satellite-manufacturing subsidiary was renamed Aspace Satellite Technology Limited in 2023.
- Investment from Alpha MBM Investments; the company in turn makes minority investments, including a stake in the private-equity trading platform Cubin LLC announced in 2023.
